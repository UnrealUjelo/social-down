## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-02

### Added

- Compose Material 3 home, downloads, and settings experiences
- Light, dark, and system theme modes
- yt-dlp URL analysis with normalized video and audio formats
- Throttled yt-dlp runtime updates with offline fallback and sanitized failure diagnostics
- Adaptive stream merging and audio conversion through bundled FFmpeg
- Background WorkManager downloads and progress notifications
- MediaStore output and Room download history
- Clipboard paste and Android Share Sheet input
- URL, path, and filename safety utilities with unit tests
- Open-source contribution, conduct, security, and automation files
